Surcharging New

Pass on the fee. Not the risk.

Merchants add a surcharge to credit card sales to cover the cost of accepting cards. Sola checks every sale against card brand rules, state rules, and the merchant's own rate before it adds a cent.

At a 3% surcharge, that's $14,400 a year. Debit, prepaid, and gift card sales don't count.

Permitted

  • Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Amex
  • Up to 3%, or 2% in Colorado
  • Never above the merchant's flat rate
  • Card present on Sola Terminal or the Sola API
  • Card not present through the Sola API
  • Disclosed on screen and on the receipt

Not permitted

  • Debit cards, even when run as credit
  • Prepaid and gift cards
  • Tax and tip: only the sale is surcharged
  • Merchants in CA, CT, MA, ME, and Puerto Rico
  • Card not present to cardholders billed there

Sola leaves the fee off, and the sale still goes through.

Who qualifies

  • Flat rate pricing, not pass-through or tiered
  • Amex OptBlue, if they take Amex
  • No convenience or service fee on the account
  • A processor that supports surcharging

New account? Turn on Enable Surcharge on the application in the Partner Portal.
