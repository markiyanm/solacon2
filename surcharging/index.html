Surcharge with confidence.

Surcharging rules change by card type, card brand, and state. Sola applies them on every transaction, so merchants surcharge compliantly without having to know the rules.

Debit and prepaid

Detects cards that can't be surcharged and skips the fee automatically.

State rules

Follows the rules where surcharging is restricted.

Rate caps

Keeps every surcharge within card brand limits.

Clear disclosure

Shows the customer the surcharge before they pay and on the receipt.

On Sola Terminal

Built into the Sola application. Turn it on and the terminal handles the rest.

Surcharging API

Partners can add the same compliant surcharging to their own solutions.
