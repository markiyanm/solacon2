What each facility sets up

  • Payment typesAdd, rename, or remove types like Monthly stay or Move-in deposit. Set an amount, leave it open, or allow a custom amount.
  • Recurring and feesOffer monthly repeat, and let the payer cover the processing fee (up to 3%).
  • Resident detailsRename the resident ID and location labels. Turn on room number and payment purpose.
  • Payer or residentAsk who pays. Family, a power of attorney, or a guardian adds the resident's name and relationship.
  • LocationsOne fixed location, or a list of properties the payer picks from on one page.
  • Payment methodsCard brands, ACH, Google Pay, and Apple Pay, as enabled on the account.
  • BrandingLogo, facility name, colors, corner style, font, and footer links and text.
  • After paymentTerms, an email or SMS receipt, a custom thank-you message, or a redirect.
