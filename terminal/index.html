Sola Terminal

Your software. Our terminal. One cloud API.

The Sola application runs on PAX payment devices and connects to any POS or software platform through the Sola cloud API.

Payment types

  • Credit and debit
  • Tap, chip, and swipe
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • EBT
  • Gift cards

Supported devices

  • PAX A920 Pro
  • PAX A80
  • PAX A35
  • PAX A77

Why a cloud API

  • Integrate once from web, desktop, or mobile software.
  • Nothing to install: no local drivers or device SDKs.
  • Smaller PCI scope: card data stays on the terminal, never in your POS.
  • Remote management: pair devices and push updates over the air.
