Why a cloud API

  • Integrate once from web, desktop, or mobile software.
  • Nothing to install: no local drivers or device SDKs.
  • Smaller PCI scope: card data stays on the terminal, never in your POS.
  • Remote management: pair devices and push updates over the air.
